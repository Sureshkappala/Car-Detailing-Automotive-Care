Paint Defect Guide

Identifying Paintwork Imperfections

Understanding the exact clear-coat defects our multi-stage correction processes safely eradicate.

🌀 Swirl Marks & Spider-Webbing

Micro-scratches caused by automated tunnel washes, dirty sponges, or improper drying towels that scatter reflected light and dull paint color.

⚡ Buffer Trails & Holograms

Ghostly, three-dimensional wave patterns left behind by untrained operators using heavy rotary buffers and abrasive compounds without finishing polish.

💧 Acid Etchings & Mineral Spots

Chemical burns in the clear coat caused by dried bird droppings, tree sap, and hard tap water minerals magnified under hot sunlight.

🌫️ Clear-Coat Oxidation & Hazing

A chalky, faded surface film caused by prolonged UV exposure and lack of hydrophobic surface protection, restored via deep compound rejuvenation.

🔥 Hard Water Spots & Limescale

Concentrated calcium and magnesium deposits baked onto horizontal hoods and roofs, dissolved via safe chemical chelation wash.

✨ Factory Orange Peel & Texture

Excess clear-coat waviness from robotic spray lines leveled using dry velvet denim polishing pads for glass-flat reflections.
