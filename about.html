Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about professional car detailing and coating protection.

Traditional carnauba wax sits on top of the clear coat and provides 4–8 weeks of shine before breaking down from heat and rain. In contrast, 9H ceramic coating forms a permanent chemical bond with your vehicle's paint, creating an inorganic sacrificial glass barrier that resists UV damage, acidic contaminants, and wash scratches for 2 to 5 years.
Paint correction eliminates 85% to 95%+ of clear-coat imperfections including swirl marks, light car wash scratches, buffer halos, and bird-dropping etchings. If a scratch has penetrated entirely through the clear coat into the primer or metal (testable if your fingernail catches inside the groove), it requires wet sanding or localized touch-up paint rather than compound buffing.
An Essential Shine wash takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. A full interior rejuvenation requires 3 to 4 hours. Multi-stage paint correction with 9H ceramic coating typically requires 1 to 2 full business days to allow proper surface decontamination, dual-stage polishing, and controlled infrared curing.
All prices shown on this site represent configurable demo starting prices for standard sedans. Actual final quotes depend on vehicle size (hatchback, sedan, SUV, luxury exotic) and existing paintwork condition, as confirmed during in-person studio check-in.
