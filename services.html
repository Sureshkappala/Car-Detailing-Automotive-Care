Science of Clean

Clinical Grade Equipment & Formulations

We deploy professional-grade chemistry and precision German/Italian equipment for paint-safe results.

🧪

pH-Neutral Snow Foams

High-lubricity surfactant formulas that encapsulate abrasive road dirt and lift grit away without stripping factory clear coats or ceramic sealants.

⚙️

Dual-Action Random Orbitals

Italian Rupes BigFoot polishers that distribute heat evenly across curved panels, preventing clear-coat burning while achieving mirror-like optical clarity.

💡

Infrared Curing Array

Short-wave infrared lamps bake the ceramic matrix into the clear-coat pores at 65°C, ensuring full molecular bonding prior to outdoor release.

💨

Heated Biocide Extraction

Commercial-grade 180°C steam injectors and sub-surface hot-water extractors eliminate allergens, mold spores, and odors without harsh chemical residue.

🔬

PosiTector Paint Gauges

Ultrasonic electromagnetic sensors non-destructively read primer, basecoat, and clear-coat layers down to 0.1 µm precision.

🛡️

SiO2 & Graphene Chemistry

Advanced quartz-silica and carbon-lattice nanocoatings that bond covalently to paint, resisting acid fallout and UV fading for years.
