The Application Protocol

The 6-Phase Ceramic Process

Proper ceramic coating is 90% preparation. Here is how we ensure flawless adhesion and longevity.

01

Chemical Decontamination

Iron-dissolving fallout bath and clay bar shaving to remove all embedded brake dust and road tar without marring the clear coat.

02

Stage 1 Heavy Compounding

Rotary machine compounding with micro-abrasive diminishing paste to level clear coat ridges and erase swirl halos.

03

Stage 2 Jeweling Finishing Polish

Ultra-fine dual-action foam pad pass to eliminate micro-marring and achieve maximum optical depth and gloss.

04

Isopropanol Alcohol Panel Wipe

Sterilizing panels with pure IPA solution to strip all polishing oils, ensuring direct covalent bonding with the clear coat.

05

Dual-Layer Ceramic Application

Crosshatch application of base quartz layer followed by a top hydrophobic gloss coat, leveling high spots under 5000K inspection.

06

Infrared Heat Curing

Short-wave infrared lamps bake every panel at 65°C for 30 minutes, locking in crystal hardness before studio departure.

Clinical Protocol

The 6-Stage Application Methodology

Every ceramic coating application is executed in a controlled clean bay under calibrated environmental conditions.

1

Iron Fallout Bath & Claying

Chemical iron dissolver removes embedded brake fallout, followed by ultra-fine synthetic clay bar treatment to remove sub-surface grit.

2

Ultrasonic Paint Depth Scan

We record 30+ clear-coat thickness measurement points across all panels to map safe polishing tolerances before any buffing machine touches the vehicle.

3

Multi-Stage Rotary Correction

Micro-abrasive polishing compounds cut away 85% to 95%+ of swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation, followed by jewel-finish refining.

4

Isopropyl Panel Prep Wipe

Every trace of polishing oil, silicone, and lubricant is chemically stripped using 70% IPA solvent wipes, leaving bare clear coat ready to bond.

5

Dual-Layer 9H Ceramic Bond

Base 9H nano-ceramic matrix is cross-hatched onto each panel, allowed to flash, and hand-leveled with plush edgeless microfibers before topcoat.

6

Infrared Heat Bake & Curing

Short-wave infrared lamps heat painted surfaces to 65°C, accelerating cross-linking so the coating is fully weatherproof upon client delivery.

Long-Term Protection

Ceramic Coating Aftercare Guidelines

Proper maintenance ensures your 9H ceramic coating delivers maximum water beading and gloss for up to 5 full years.

⏳

First 7 Days Curing

Avoid washing the vehicle with shampoo or detergents for the first 7 days while the nanotech matrix reaches full 9H pencil hardness.

🧼

Use pH-Neutral Shampoos Only

Avoid high-pH commercial touchless car washes with harsh caustic degreasers. Use ceramic-infused pH-neutral maintenance wash soaps.

🛡️

Annual Ceramic Health Check

Visit AutoShine once a year for an iron decontamination wash and hydrophobic SiO2 silica spray sealant top-up to maintain warranty coverage.
