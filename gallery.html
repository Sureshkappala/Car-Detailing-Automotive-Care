The Clean Bay Technology Behind the Finish

Every photo in our gallery is illuminated under high-intensity inspection arrays designed to expose paint flaws rather than hide them.

💡

CRI 98+ Scangrip LED Arrays

Full-spectrum inspection lighting reproduces true daylight color temperatures (4500K to 6500K), revealing micro-holograms invisible under normal garage lights.

🌪️

HEPA Positive Pressure Clean Bay

Our dedicated ceramic application bay cycles filtered air continuously, preventing microscopic airborne dust from settling during coating flash times.

Non-Invasive Paint Depth Scanners

PosiTector electromagnetic paint thickness gauges measure clear-coat depth down to 0.1 microns, ensuring surgical safety on exotic carbon and aluminum panels.
