Frequently Asked Automotive Care Questions

Everything you need to know about our paint correction, ceramic coatings, and studio care standards.

Traditional carnauba wax sits on top of your paintwork as an organic layer that washes away within 4 to 8 weeks, especially under heat and road detergent exposure. A 9H nano-ceramic coating chemically cross-links with the clear coat at a molecular level, forming a semi-permanent, glass-like quartz shield that resists UV degradation, bird lime etching, and high-temperature brake dust for up to 5 full years.
No. Our certified master technicians use non-invasive dual-action orbital polishers and digital ultrasonic thickness gauges. A typical multi-stage correction removes only 1.5 to 2.5 microns of clear coat (less than the thickness of a single post-it note), while permanently eliminating 85% to 95%+ of surface swirls and scratches.
Thanks to the extreme hydrophobic self-cleaning effect, washing becomes effortless. Simply rinse with high-pressure water to dislodge loose grit, wash using a dedicated pH-neutral shampoo and two-bucket grit guard method, and dry with an ultra-plush 500+ GSM microfiber towel or filtered warm-air blower.
